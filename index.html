    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    What services does Element Design offer?

    Element Design covers architecture, interior design (villas, private houses, residences, cottages, mansions, penthouses and apartments), design for business (hotels, restaurants, resorts, offices, spa and wellness, common areas and bars), BIM engineering, design supervision, construction management and procurement. See the full list on our services page.

    Does Element Design take care of the whole project?

    Yes. We can take over the full cycle of project implementation: architectural design, interior design and engineering solutions, so you receive a fully implemented project without having to dive into technical processes.

    How do I start a project with Element Design?

    Fill out the contact form with your name and current phone number. Our personal expert will contact you to discuss the requirements of your project and introduce our approach. After a detailed review of your wishes, we prepare a personalized project plan and a tailored offer.

    Will I have a personal contact at the studio?

    Yes. From the moment the project starts you are assigned a personal Element Design expert who coordinates the work of architects, designers and engineers, so every decision works as part of one unified system.

    Is every design created individually?

    Every Element Design project is created exclusively for you, starting from individual sketches, your lifestyle scenarios, and your taste and chosen references. Nothing is taken from a catalogue. You can see examples in our portfolio.

    How is the budget for my project determined?

    The budget depends on the area of the property, the level of individualization and the composition of services. After the first meeting with your personal expert you receive a detailed commercial proposal with a complete budget structure.

    What are the stages of an Element Design project?

    Each project follows a defined system of stages: technical specification, planning solution, selection of references, 3D visualization, working documentation and author supervision. You always know the current stage and the next steps.

    Can I follow the progress of construction from another city or country?

    Yes. Clients get access to a project reporting system from anywhere in the world: you can monitor the progress of construction, receive photos and videos from the site and get regular reports.

    Where does Element Design work?

    The studio is based in Mumbai, India, and has completed projects across India. Read more about the studio and its founders on our about page.

    Who founded Element Design?

    Element Design was founded in 2017 by Yogesh Mewada and Nitish Tiwari. You can read about the studio and its recognition on our about page and in the blog.
